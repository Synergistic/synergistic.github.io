# Rivetstorm Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-11-02 *(update this date whenever the policy changes)*

Rivetstorm ("the game") is made by **Cinderous Works** ("we", "us").
Contact: **cinderous.works@protonmail.com**

This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and statistics **on your device only**. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads, for example to revive once during a run. These ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
- your device's advertising ID;
- your IP address;
- device and app information (such as model, operating system and app version);
- your interactions with ads;
- your approximate location (country, region or city), estimated from your IP address.

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** Google's consent form asks for your choice the first time you play. Personalized ads are shown only if you agree.
- **Elsewhere:** Google may show personalized ads according to its own policies and your device settings.
- **iPhone/iPad:** ads are always non-personalized, and the game does not track you across other companies' apps.
- You can review or change your ad choices at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Purchases → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## 3. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

To balance and improve the game, we use **GameAnalytics** to collect anonymous gameplay events. These include runs started and finished, waves reached, in-game shop choices, ads offered or watched, and purchases made. GameAnalytics also receives basic device information (such as device model, operating system, app version and a random device identifier), and uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location (country, region or city); GameAnalytics keeps the IP address only in shortened form, for up to 30 days. We do not send your name, email address or contacts.

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** statistics are only sent if you agree.
- **Elsewhere:** they are sent unless you turn them off.
- Change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Purchases → Privacy options**.

GameAnalytics privacy policy: <https://gameanalytics.com/privacy-policy>

## 4. Crash reports (Sentry)

If the game crashes or hits an error, it can send a crash report to **Sentry** so we can fix it. A report contains technical details: the error and where in the game it happened, the device model, operating system, app version, and a random install identifier. It does not include your name, email or IP address (we have turned IP storage off). Crash reports follow the same choice as gameplay statistics: they are sent only when statistics are allowed (see section 3).

Sentry privacy policy: <https://sentry.io/privacy/>

## 5. Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores to Google so they can appear on leaderboards. Google handles your Play Games player ID and display name under its own privacy policy. Leaderboards are optional; the game works without signing in.

Google privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 6. Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Supporter" pack is sold through **Google Play**. Google handles your payment details; we never see them. The game only learns which products your account owns, so it can unlock and restore them.

## 7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Change ad and statistics choices in **Settings → Privacy & Purchases** at any time.
- Delete all local game data by uninstalling the game.
- To ask about, access or delete data connected to you, email **cinderous.works@protonmail.com**. Data held by Google, GameAnalytics and Sentry is governed by their policies (linked above). We will help you contact them where we can.
- Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, such as the right to object or to complain to a data-protection authority.

## 9. Security

Data sent by the game to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game adds or removes a service. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be noted in the game's update notes.
